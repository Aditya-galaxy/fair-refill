# What a month of a common generic costs

Measured 2026-10-20 by `tools/measure_savings.py`, from the sources Fair Refill answers from: CMS NADAC (what pharmacies pay), the Cost Plus Drugs public API (with its $5.25 shipping an order added) and Amazon's RxPass list. Every tablet and capsule strength of the generics the add-on keeps ready.

- **37 common generics**, every tablet and capsule strength RxNorm lists: 275 in all, 30 a month.
- **Cost Plus Drugs sells 35 of 37 (95%) of the generics**, and 164 of 275 (60%) of the strengths. RxNorm lists every approved product, including rarely used ones, so the strengths it doesn't sell are mostly the unusual ones.
- Where it does, a month delivered costs **$11.21 at the median**, and **144 of 164 (88%) cost under $15**. The dearest are unusual products, and this prices everything at 30 a month, so a once-a-week capsule is overstated; the table shows each.
- Three months in one fill comes to **$4.38 a month at the median**; 124 of 164 (76%) come under $5 a month.
- Amazon RxPass's list may cover **62 of 275 (23%)** ($5 a month with Prime, outside insurance; Amazon doesn't list strengths).
- Not sold by Cost Plus in any strength: apixaban, gabapentin.

These are prices, not what anyone pays: copays vary by plan, and no public source lists them. Someone whose copay is above the delivered price for their medicine is who the add-on is for.
